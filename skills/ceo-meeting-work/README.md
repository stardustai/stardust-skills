# CEO Meeting Work

Handles meeting preparation, minutes, transcripts, silent meetings, summaries, decisions, action items, and follow-up information.

Use this skill for meeting content after attendance is settled. Use `ceo-calendar-invite` for attendance decisions and `ceo-work-tracking` when an agreed action becomes durable tracked work.

Key boundaries:

- Load the meeting operation skill for the source platform, such as `dingtalk-minutes`, `lark-vc`, or `lark-minutes`.
- Read meeting identity and summary first, then tasks, transcript, and linked material as needed.
- Do not infer meeting content from a title, preview, link, or old conclusion.
- Do not assign work without evidence tied to the participant and task.
- Ask for one specific missing material when decisive evidence is unavailable.
