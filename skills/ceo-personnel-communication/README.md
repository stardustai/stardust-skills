# CEO Personnel Communication

Handles people-sensitive communication involving employees, candidates, interviews, performance, compensation, promotion, role fit, and audience visibility.

Use this skill when the subject, recipient, audience, or visibility changes what may be read, judged, or communicated. Ordinary business ownership or delivery facts should stay in the relevant business workflow unless the requested judgment is about personnel status.

Key boundaries:

- Identify the information subject and the actual destination before proposing communication.
- Establish whether the recipient is authorized to receive the supported facts.
- Load specialist workflows such as `stardust-interview`, `dingtalk-oa-approval`, or `dingtang-okr-review` only when their domain applies.
- Do not disclose unsupported compensation, performance, promotion, employment, health, leave, or sensitive candidate facts.
- A person's name alone does not make ordinary business facts personnel information.
