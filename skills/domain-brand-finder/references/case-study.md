# Historical research crosswalk and reusable examples

This reference explains how the supplied research influenced the workflow. It is not a list of available brands or a claim that naming caused company success.

## Twelve historical patterns: where each belongs

| Historical pattern | Proper axis | Current catalog location | How to retain it without double counting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Myth/literary proper name | Material | S5 | Borrow the actual name, or explicitly record a transformation |
| Abstract cognition word | Material | S4 | Borrow/clip/derive/etc.; abstraction is not an operation |
| Blend | Operation | T11/T12 | State which segments merge or disappear |
| Clipping | Operation | T9 | State the longer input and retained portion |
| Pure sound invention | Operation | T14 | Coin; no fabricated ancient meaning; check pool permission |
| Respelled real word | Operation | T10 | Respell; record hearing-to-spelling costs |
| Place name | Material | S6 | Borrow or transform a verified place name |
| Scientist/unit name | Material | S7 | Borrow or transform; verify referent and attribution |
| One-syllable impact word | Sound | Sound/form section | Apply to selected source/operation, or define an explicit display row |
| Two-syllable brand shell | Sound | Sound/form section | Apply to selected source/operation, or define an explicit display row |
| Personal name as institution | Material/association | S8 | Borrow, with persona and rights/association review |
| Foreign word sound | Material plus sound | S9 | Verify original language and separate adaptation operation |

Catalog IDs refer to [the integrated creative framework](creative-framework.md). Added options include ordinary objects/nature/actions (S1–S3), concrete prefix/suffix and ending experiments (T1–T8), and full-word compounds (T13). These expand, rather than erase, the historical directions. Sound-based display rows can have their own requested five examples, but each spelling fills one primary row only. A closed vocabulary pool may leave special sources pending permission; do not silently delete those routes or treat the skill's catalog as permission to use them.

Whole-word compounds and derivation were missing as clear standalone operations in that historical checklist. Adding them repairs a useful working taxonomy; it does not make the new seven labels the only conceivable ways to name a brand.

## How to analyze a name without inventing founder intent

The following are **form analyses** unless a linked primary source establishes the company's own account. They are not candidates for reuse and have not been screened here.

| Example | Observable construction / lesson | What not to infer |
|---|---|---|
| Cursor / Figure | Familiar whole word borrowed into a new product context | Every short dictionary word will be commercially accessible |
| HumanLayer / Databricks | Transparent full components can imply architecture or material | Two meaningful words automatically create a natural whole |
| Morningstar | An existing compound word can carry a scene; borrowing a lexicalized whole is different from newly combining words | The company invented the English word, or contrast caused its success |
| Lyft | Visible spelling change relative to lift; spelling repair is part of the tradeoff | Respellings remove near-sound trademark risks |
| Shopify | Productive-looking shop + -ify form is analyzable as derivation | This analysis alone establishes the founder's historical reasoning |
| Gumloop | Company-authored account discusses the AgentHub-to-Gumloop change | One successful rebrand proves a universal playful-compound rule |

For Gumloop's own account see [Why we rebranded to Gumloop](https://www.gumloop.com/blog/agenthub-to-gumloop), checked 2026-09-14. Refresh factual historical claims and links when using the case. For other examples, do not add an origin story without a primary source.

## Lessons from the Friday case (not defaults for new projects)

The supplied marketing package treated naming as a coherent product-family decision, not an isolated logo. Its useful implementation consequence is to test real combinations and usage sentences. Its old names, Chinese-name requirement and handoff instructions remain historical material, subordinate to the current user's English-only product brief.

The supplied 500-company map covered a bounded sample (predominantly YC 2024–2025 cohorts). Its categories mixed sources, semantic effects and word forms. The upgrade uses the sample as a craft reference, separates those axes, and avoids causal success claims. Do not export its category proportions as the distribution of all AI companies.

Friday's earlier ten-name result had nine new complete-word combinations, even though generation teams had different semantic labels. This motivated operation-based assignments, survivor-template auditing and a names-only first review. A rejected obscure foreign word motivated attention to immediate feeling; it did **not** authorize a blanket ban on foreign sources, abstraction or coinage.
