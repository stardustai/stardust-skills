# Independent quality review — rubric v3

Freeze this version before a run. Preserve historical scores without conversion.

| Dimension key | Weight | What earns points |
|---|---:|---|
| first_impression | 25 | Immediate feeling and fit with the specified route, before explanation |
| distinctiveness | 20 | Distinctive yet natural construction, not merely unfamiliar spelling |
| meaning_fit | 20 | Honest, useful product meaning and concise explanation |
| english_transmission | 15 | Predicted English pronunciation, spelling and oral transmission |
| family_extension | 10 | Works with the project's actual module/product family |
| visual_narrative | 10 | A usable visual or narrative direction beyond decorative wordplay |

Each dimension needs a reason and a deduction. A rough anchor: 50% weak; 70% ordinary; 85% strong with a meaningful limitation; 95% exceptional with concrete support. Do not award near-perfect numbers by default. A one-point difference is not statistical evidence.

The principal reviewer is distinct from the generator. Subagent self-scores never establish passage. If independent review is unavailable, disclose it rather than claim it occurred. In Friday, the principal/root agent reviews Luna's candidates.

## Order of review

Creative comparison precedes this formal scoring path. Follow [creative-framework.md](creative-framework.md): default to qualitative names-first reactions and five examples per agreed row (or the user's count), not five automatic 90s. Do not start public-use/domain/trademark screening before a requested creative-alignment decision. Dictionary/source verification is not commercial clearance. Previously approved scope need not be reconfirmed.

1. **Names only**: record first association and route fit before reading etymologies or marketing copy.
2. Check actual operation and any attested definition/source. Keep original meaning separate from the creator's association.
3. Predict pronunciation/stress, likely mishearing, spelling repair, and negative associations. These remain desk predictions.
4. Once screening is authorized and any requested creative alignment is complete, check exact public product usage before the formal review pool. Record collisions separately from creative scores; this prerequisite does not apply to the earlier creative comparison.
5. Test product usage sentences and every configured family suffix. State any awkward combinations.
6. Assign six scores with evidence and deductions. Domain availability and trademark risk are separate gates, never compensating points.
7. Audit constructions and repeated templates by route; perform a real missing-method comparison where indicated.

For a threshold run, names below the fixed threshold are rejected for this run, not rounded up because too few survive. Do not use the target number to decide scores. An unavailable excellent name can remain in the rejection log; an obtainable weak name cannot replace it in the formal shortlist.

Unscored early triage is “not advanced at this checkpoint; no v3 score assigned,” not a numerical below-threshold verdict. If a borderline rejection is challenged, complete the six-dimensional review against the same accepted/rejected anchors. Unless the brief requires literal naming, an indirect metaphor is not an extra disqualifying gate: evaluate how concise and useful its bridge is, without erasing independently strong imagery. Keep public-use identity findings out of quality arithmetic. Preserve the original first impression and any prior score; record an explained reconsideration separately. Calibration can confirm rejection and must never be a way to fill a quantity target.

## Human and legal testing boundary

Without actual participants, mark human testing **NOT_DONE**. Later testing may include blind first association, heard-once spelling, delayed recall, and realistic product usage with the target audience. Record who participated, when, sample size and actual protocol/results; do not invent any of them.

Agent US screening is not lawyer clearance. Mark legal clearance **NOT_DONE** unless a separately documented professional review exists; the bundled preliminary-only validator does not certify that external process. “90” is an internal quality threshold, not a probability of commercial success, registrability or absence of disputes.
